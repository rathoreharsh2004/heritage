About Rathore Heritage Developers

Architecture That Remembers.

Rathore Heritage Developers specializes in authentic heritage construction with deep expertise in traditional architectural craftsmanship. We design luxurious heritage spaces including havelis, villas, resorts, and palace-style interiors that reflect royal elegance and cultural authenticity.

Our projects feature signature elements like Heritage Dodi entrances, Thekri glass work, heritage-style bars, intricately carved furniture, decorative pillars, and traditional Jhomer chandeliers. Ceilings are artistically detailed with Mor Pankh (peacock feather) themes, along with authentic Pipla Patti and Tordi craftsmanship.

We also design Ghokda-style domes and marble borders with Khajur Patti detailing, preserving the true essence of heritage architecture. By blending traditional artistry with strong civil engineering practices, we ensure strength, durability, and timeless beauty in every project.
